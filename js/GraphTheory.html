Graceful Labeling

History: Graceful Labelings of graphs were first studied by Alexander Rosa. He first published a paper, "On certain valuations of the vertices of a graph", on graceful labelings in 1967, although he originally called it Β-valuations.
Definition: A $graceful$ $labeling$ is a labeling of a graph with $n$ edges in which:
  • the vertices are labeled with a subset of distinct integers from the set $\{0, 1, 2, \dots, n\}$,
  • the edges are labeled with a subset of distinct integers from the set $ \{1, 2, \dots, n\},$
  • each edge label is the absolute difference of its vertex endpoint labels,
  • vertex and edge labelings are unique.
